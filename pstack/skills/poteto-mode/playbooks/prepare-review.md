### Prepare a local review

1. Review the scoped diff against the available local base. Keep unrelated work out and respect the repository's branch and commit rules.
2. Run the checks that demonstrate the behavior. Use the real local app or CLI when the change needs it. Report any check you could not run.
3. Hand back the diff or local branch, the change's intent, evidence, and open risks. When asked for a PR description, draft it in the reply or at a requested local path.

**Reply:** what changed, what verifies it, and where to review the work.
